Cutout

Add a photo to remove its background

Works on plain backdrops, green screens, or busy real-world photos. Paint out watermarks, then compress and save — it all happens on your device, nothing is uploaded anywhere.

You can also drop or paste them. Pick more than one for batch cut-out and compression. JPG, PNG and WebP work.

Pick Transparent to keep a see-through background, or a colour to also bake it into the image you save.

Background type

Plain colour is fastest for a green screen or solid backdrop. Photo / complex follows the edge of your subject and works for busy, photographic backgrounds.

Colours to remove

Up to 4

    Green is found for you. To remove another colour too, choose Add colour, then touch it in the image. Auto-detect starts over with the colour along the edges. Type or paste a code into a colour's box, or use the copy icon to grab it.

    Refine the cut

    Smart background removal

    Best for real photos with a busy background, like the hose coils example. It follows whatever is connected to the edges of the image, tracking colour changes as it goes rather than using a fixed colour list, clears out small leftover speckles automatically, and refines the final edge against your photo's own detail rather than just blurring it.

    Clean up & watermark removal

    Erase paints over leftover marks. Restore brings erased areas back. Paint covers a watermark or blemish with a solid colour you choose — pick it with the swatch or paste a code. Choose Move to scroll around when zoomed in.
